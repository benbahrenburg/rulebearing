What the numbers can and cannot say:

- **The first three signals measure almost nothing yet.** The script detects agent work from the pull request's author or a `Co-Authored-By` trailer, and this repository's agent work carries neither, so 70 of 71 merged pull requests read as human. One pull request is not a rate. [docs/adoption.md](../adoption.md) names this gap as the first to close.
- **Two consecutive months have not been measured.** The baseline is from 2026-09-23; this is the first review after it.
- **The private monorepo,** the baseline that matters most, can only be measured by the owner, and has not been.
- **The last three signals are met.** That includes the Stop-hook p95, which became measurable with wave 3.

## The funding outcome

**The entry criterion is not met on the evidence.** Four signals meet their targets on one repository. The first two, the ones the decision rule turns on, have one data point between them, and nothing has been measured over two consecutive months.

**The wave is funded by the owner's decision of 2026-10-10** ("move onto implementing plan 0004-wave-4-reach.md"), ahead of the entry criterion. The decision rule of § 1.2 is therefore not what funds it. It stays the record of what the signals would have decided. The closing review (Step 14) measures the signals again, and says whether the agent features earned their place.

| Sub-wave | Funded | By |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4A docs site, playground, cookbook, live tables | yes | the owner's decision |
| 4B pull-request app and Azure DevOps extension | no, deferred | the owner's rule of 2026-09-30 (§ 1.2): it waits until the product ships, meaning plans 0001 to 0003 are implemented and a non-placeholder release is on every registry. Publishing is the owner's, still to come |
| 4C `fix --plan` and the eval harness | yes | the owner's decision, which the § 1.2 rule alone would not give: the first two signals have not moved because they have not been measured |
| 4D `fleet` and declared edges | yes | the owner's decision |
| 4E usage counts and the closing review | yes | never cut (§ 3) |
